How it runs

  1. Agents draft. You approve here. Nothing is sent, posted or applied for without you.
  2. Every lane keeps its to-do list and history in the Tasks table. Agents add to it, and so can you.
  3. "Hand to Claude" sends a task to the worker agent, which does it and reports back here.
  4. Opus writes anything in your voice. Sonnet handles watching, sorting and reports.
  5. Repeat fetching (job boards, events, tracks) moves to free scripts over time.
